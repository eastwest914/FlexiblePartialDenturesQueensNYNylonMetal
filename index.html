Flexible partial dentures are removable replacements for missing teeth made from a lightweight, flexible material such as nylon. They typically need no metal clasps, so they can look more natural and feel more comfortable.

Many patients find metal-free dentures lighter and more comfortable than traditional metal-framework partials. A custom fit is key, and we design each denture around your mouth.
